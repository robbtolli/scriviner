# Privacy Policy for Scrivener

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scrivener ("we," "our," or "the application") handles your information. We believe your gaming data should remain private and under your control.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Scrivener does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, physical addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
- **No Telemetry or Analytics:** We do not run tracking software, behavioral analytics, session recorders, or advertising frameworks.
- **No Account Creation:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide credentials to use the application.

---

## 2. Information You Create and Local Storage

Scrivener is designed as a local-first application:

- **Game Data:** Any information entered into the application—such as Dungeons & Dragons (D&D) character sheets, stats, spells, inventory, backstory notes, and campaign logs—is stored strictly locally on your device.
- **No Remote Servers:** We do not operate cloud servers, external databases, or remote backups for your character data. Your information never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export, back up, or share your local files yourself.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively on your local storage:

- You retain complete ownership and control of your files at all times.
- To delete your character data, you can delete individual characters inside the app, clear the application’s local storage/cache, or uninstall the application from your device.
- We cannot restore lost or deleted data, as we do not have access to or copies of your files.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services and Code Hosting

- **Source Repository:** The application source code is publicly hosted on GitHub ([https://github.com/robbtolli/scriviner](https://github.com/robbtolli/scriviner)). If you interact with the repository directly (e.g., submitting issues, pull requests, or participating in discussions), your interactions are governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
- **Third-Party APIs:** The application does not send your character data to any third-party APIs or external cloud services.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Scrivener does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction). It is entirely safe for users of all ages to create and manage character sheets.

---

## 6. Security

Because your data is stored locally, the security of your characters and notes depends on the security settings of your personal device and operating system (such as device passwords, encryption, and local file access permissions).

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the application’s data architecture changes in the future (for instance, if optional cloud synchronization features are introduced), this policy will be updated accordingly in the project repository prior to those features going live.

---
